── PHASE 17 — OOO Coverage ─────────────────────────────────────────────────

When a colleague is out, you can now load their campaigns directly inside
Stella — no spreadsheet access needed, no switching accounts.

Click "Cover for AM" in the sidebar, enter their SXM email, and Stella
loads their full campaign database with read/write access. An amber banner
at the top of the campaigns view shows you're in coverage mode, and an
Exit button returns you to your own campaigns instantly.

Optionally attach a coverage document to give Stella context about what's
happening with the absent AM's accounts. You can either paste notes directly
into the modal, or link a Google Doc (it must be shared with your SiriusXM
Google account). Stella reads the document server-side and uses it when
answering questions — no copy/paste into chat required.

While you're covering:
- Stella's answers are grounded in the covered AM's campaign data
- She references their campaigns by name and flags issues specific to
  their book, not yours
- Any edits, saves, or deletes you make go directly into the covered
  AM's campaign database tab
- The coverage document context is session-only — nothing is saved after
  you exit or close the tab


── PHASE 1 — The Beginning ─────────────────────────────────────────────────

Started as a single-purpose billing review tool. You could paste billing
data into a text box and chat with an AI about it. That's it — but it
proved the core idea worked: an AI that understands SXM's billing context
and can answer specific questions about your data.


── PHASE 2 — Stella Is Born ────────────────────────────────────────────────

The billing tool grew into a full operating partner. This is when Stella
got her name, her personality, and her purpose.

Key additions:
- Stella knows your campaigns automatically — no need to paste data every
  time. She reads your campaign database and uses it to answer questions.
- The Billing Knowledge Base was wired in — Stella understands IO approval,
  OMS setup, billing close, make-goods, 3P reporting, and escalation paths.
- Every AM gets their own tab in a shared Google Sheet, created automatically
  on first login. No setup required.


── PHASE 3 — Campaign Management ───────────────────────────────────────────

Stella became your campaign dashboard, not just a chat window.

- Add, edit, and delete campaigns through a form (21 fields covering
  everything from Slingshot IDs to IO Case URLs).
- Campaign cards with color-coded status badges and pacing bars that turn
  red when a campaign is under 80%.
- Campaign detail view: all your platform links in one place (Slingshot,
  OMS, ARIA, Salesforce, IO Case, DAM folder, Castle), plus all your
  tickets auto-linked to JIRA.
- Flight warnings: Stella alerts you 7 days before a flight ends.
- Inline Stella chat on every campaign detail — she already knows the
  campaign you're looking at when you ask her something.


── PHASE 4 — Home Dashboard & Settings ────────────────────────────────────

A real home base to start your day from.

- Dashboard with five widgets: Campaign Calendar, Pending Tasks, Meetings
  (placeholder), Quick Links, and the Stella Runner mini-game.
- Dark mode, accent color choices, and archive settings all saved per device.
- Widget toggles — show or hide whatever you don't need.


── PHASE 5 & 6 — Virtual Office ────────────────────────────────────────────

Six agent desks representing Stella's full vision: Billing, Stella,
Trafficking, Reporting, Handoff, and Tagging.

- Stella's desk is live — click it to open a full chat session.
- The other agents show what's coming and why.
- Two views: a polished card layout and a pixel-art office scene where
  Stella appears as the SXM dog mascot. Toggle between them.


── PHASE 7 — Dashboard Redesign ────────────────────────────────────────────

The dashboard got a complete overhaul.

- Drag-and-drop widget reorder — arrange your dashboard however you want,
  and it stays that way.
- Each widget has a close button to hide it without going to Settings.
- Campaign Calendar redesigned as a Gantt chart (today ±90 days) with
  color-coded bars. Toggle to a compact sortable list view.
- Quick Links upgraded: added Ads Manager, The Hub, and 7 CS Hub tools
  (Ratecard, Podcast Explorer, Audience Discovery, etc.). Show or hide
  any individual link with the gear button.


── PHASE 8 — Campaign Management Upgrades ──────────────────────────────────

More power, less manual work.

- Structured flight date picker: add individual flight start/end date pairs
  instead of typing free text. Past entries auto-convert.
- 1P Billing and 3P Billing checkboxes added to every campaign.
- List view added to campaigns — toggle between card grid and sortable table.
- Seller view: share a read-only link with your sellers so they can see
  your campaigns without edit access. "Copy Seller Link" button in the sidebar.
- Auto schema migration: new fields added to your Sheet tab automatically —
  no manual spreadsheet work ever needed.


── PHASE 9 & 10 — Inline Editing & Resources ──────────────────────────────

Faster edits and a one-stop hub for your tools.

- Per-section inline editing on campaign detail: update just pacing, or
  just platform IDs, or just tickets — without opening the full modal.
- Resources tab: all your Quick Links, CS Tools, and custom resources in
  one browseable page. Add your own links with a custom label, URL, icon,
  and color. Edit or delete them anytime.


── PHASE 11 — Morning Briefing & Dashboard Upgrades ───────────────────────

Stella starts proactively watching your campaigns.

- Daily morning briefing: once per day when you open Stella, she reads
  your campaign data and writes a personalized health check in her own
  voice. Flights ending soon, low pacing, live campaigns without traffic
  tickets — she surfaces it all so you don't have to go looking.
- Campaign Activity widget: a notification feed of live alerts. Click any
  alert to see a quick-links popup for that campaign. Dismiss alerts
  individually or all at once.
- To-Do widget: a personal task list with optional due dates and campaign
  tie-ins. Completed items can auto-archive after a set number of days.
- Seller link reliability fix: the shared seller URL now survives Google's
  auth redirect reliably.


── PHASE 12 — Onboarding & Guided Tour ────────────────────────────────────

A proper welcome for new users.

- On first login, Stella asks what to call you. Your name is used in every
  greeting and briefing from that point on.
- A 6-step guided tour walks you through every section with a spotlight
  and tooltip explaining what's live and what's coming.
- The tour is restartable from Settings, useful for demos or onboarding
  new team members.


── PHASE 13 — Column Customization & Bug Fixes ────────────────────────────

Make the campaign list view your own.

- Add, remove, reorder, and resize columns in list view. All 20 campaign
  fields are available as columns — including Salesforce, IO Case, Castle,
  DAM Folder, tickets, billing flags, and more. Link columns open directly
  in a new tab.
- Fixed a bug where clicking into campaigns or hitting Edit in list view
  was broken when campaign names or notes contained special characters.
- What's New popup: auto-shows when there's a new build, and always
  accessible from the sidebar so you can catch up on what changed.


── PHASE 14 — Outlook Calendar Integration ────────────────────────────────

Your Meetings widget is now live.

- Connect your Outlook calendar by running /refresh-stella-calendar in
  Claude Code. It fetches your next 7 days of meetings and pushes them
  to Stella.
- The Meetings widget groups events by day (Today, Tomorrow, then by date)
  and shows time, meeting name, and location for each event.
- The footer shows how long ago you last synced, so you always know if
  it's current. Re-run the command anytime to refresh.


── PHASE 15 — Team Views, Roles & Seller Login ────────────────────────────

Stella now works for your whole team, not just individual AMs.

On first login, Stella asks your role:

  Account Manager
  Your experience is unchanged. Everything works exactly as before.

  Team Lead / Manager
  Enter your AMs' email addresses once during setup (or anytime from
  Settings). From then on:
  - Your Campaigns page shows all team campaigns in one view, labeled
    by AM. Filter down to a specific rep with the AM dropdown.
  - Open any campaign and edit it — same full access as any AM.
  - Stella's morning briefing covers your whole team: she names specific
    AMs when flagging low pacing, ending flights, or untrafficked campaigns.
  - Ask Stella anything about the team: pacing health, billing status,
    incremental revenue opportunities. She has context on every campaign
    across your roster.

  Seller
  Log in with your SXM Google account — no special link needed. Stella
  automatically finds all campaigns where your name appears in the Sales
  Rep field, across all AMs on the platform. You'll see which AM owns each
  campaign, but can't edit anything.


── PHASE 16 — 3P Pacing Sheets ────────────────────────────────────────────

Track third-party delivery campaign by campaign, directly inside Stella.

The Pacing tab now has three sub-tabs: 3P (live), 1P, and Programmatic
(both coming in future phases).

For the 3P tab, you can create a pacing sheet for any campaign in your
database. The sheet is organized by flight — each flight period becomes a
collapsible section header showing the start and end dates, total days,
days remaining, and expected pacing percentage at the current point in time.

Under each flight you add placement rows. The fields you enter are:

  LID, Placement name, Goal impressions, Ad Server, 1P to Date, 3P to Date, Notes

Everything else calculates automatically:

  Expected to Date — how many impressions should have delivered by today
  DFP % to Goal    — 1P delivery as a percentage of the goal
  3P % to Goal     — 3P delivery as a percentage of the goal
  Expected %       — how far through the flight you are today
  DFP Remaining    — impressions still needed to hit goal
  Discrepancy      — difference between 1P and 3P delivery

Pacing percentages are color-coded green, amber, or red relative to where
you should be in the flight.

Editing works like a spreadsheet: click any editable cell, type the value,
and it saves automatically when you click away. The Last Updated date
auto-stamps whenever you update a 1P or 3P delivery number.

When creating a new pacing sheet (or adding a flight), Stella checks whether
your campaign already has structured flight dates. If it does, those dates
appear as one-click chips so you can fill in the start and end without
typing. You can also enter any custom date range manually.

A "3P Pacing" button on every campaign detail view takes you straight to
that campaign's sheet without going through the Pacing tab manually.

Pacing data is stored in the existing campaign database — no extra Sheet
tabs, no new files. It scales cleanly regardless of how many AMs are using
Stella.
